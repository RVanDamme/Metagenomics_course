Galaxy route

Each practical of the course can be done in two ways: on your laptop (the local tutorials of this site) or on Galaxy, with tutorials of the Galaxy Training Network (GTN).

The whole class follows the same route. For each practical, the teacher says which route we use. If we have to change route during a practical (for example because a server is slow), everybody changes together and we start the other route from its beginning.

The Galaxy tutorials use their own data, not the data of the local tutorials, unless the table says “same data”. The methods are the same; the numbers are not.

Before the first Galaxy practical

  1. Create an account on usegalaxy.eu and confirm your e-mail address (see Setup).
  2. Join the training group of the course with the link given on the Setup page.
  3. Follow the short introduction to Galaxy.

What differs between the two routes

  • Databases. A result depends on the reference database and on its version. The local Day 1 tutorial uses the Kraken 2 database Standard-8 of October 2026. The Galaxy tutorial uses a larger database (PlusPF), and the Standard-8 database on usegalaxy.eu dates from June 2022. Expect different numbers, and always write down the database and its version.
  • Tools. The Galaxy binning tutorial uses several binners and refines their bins; the local tutorial uses one binner (MetaBAT 2). The local DADA2 tutorial uses SILVA 138.2; the Galaxy DADA2 tutorial uses the SILVA release installed on Galaxy (132), which still has the old names of the phyla (Firmicutes, Bacteroidetes).
  • Waiting time. On Galaxy your jobs wait in a queue with the jobs of other users. Start the long jobs before a break.

The GTN tutorials are updated regularly. The links were checked on 12 November 2026.
